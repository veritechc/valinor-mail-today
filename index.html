Mail Today

A private, self-hosted viewer for USPS Informed Delivery.

Purpose

Valinor Mail Today helps its owner review expected USPS mailpieces and associated envelope scans from a private household dashboard.

The application connects to a dedicated Gmail account using Google OAuth and the read-only Gmail permission. It processes only matching USPS Informed Delivery messages.

Private by design

The dashboard runs on a private server and is protected by authentication and HTTPS. It is not offered as a public service.

Limited access

Google data is used only to identify USPS delivery messages, display reported counts, and store valid mailpiece scans.

Automatic retention

Processed delivery records and images are automatically removed after 30 days.
